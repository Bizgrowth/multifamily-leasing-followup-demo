# Demo data source

**File:** `linden-park-synthetic-workbook.xlsx`
**Type:** synthetic (fallback). No suitable public multifamily dataset exists with monthly property financials and a leasing box score in one file.
**Made by:** `make_synthetic_workbook.py` in this folder, with a fixed random seed so it rebuilds the same way every time.
**Licence:** created for this demo by AI Ops Expert. No third-party data.

## What's in it
- One fictional property: "Linden Park", 268 units, calendar year 2025.
- Monthly financial summary and detail rows (income, operating expenses, controllable NOI, and others), with an original budget.
- Monthly leasing box score: calls, emails, texts, walk-ins, visits, applications, and prospects.
- One planted difference to show the reconciliation check working: July operating expenses are $12,416.27 lower in the summary than in the detail rows.

## What's not in it
No real property, company, client, or vendor names. No people, emails, phone numbers, or addresses. No figures copied from any real business. The four prospects in the follow-up lab (Jordan, Riley, Morgan, Taylor, IDs DEMO-101 to DEMO-104) are fictional and live in the demo page, not in this file.
